IBOX 2.3.2

IBOX account (Telegram)
- Official "Log In with Telegram": the main button opens Telegram's own sign-in page. "Use the IBOX bot instead" keeps the bot sign-in.
- One sign-in for everything: music opens with your IBOX account, with no second Telegram sign-in and no required music password.
- My subscription and Music now show the same pages as the Telegram mini apps, inside IBOX (Windows and Android).
