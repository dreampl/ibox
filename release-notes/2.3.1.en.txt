IBOX 2.3.1

IBOX account (Telegram)
- Signing in is offered on first launch and is optional. Without it IBOX works as before with any subscription you add.
- Music and the in-app subscription manager need the sign-in. Sign in or out any time from Profiles.
- After you sign in, the subscriptions on your Telegram account are added to IBOX automatically. One you delete is not added again.
- New buttons for My subscription and Music on the home screen.

IBAR
- A faded notch opens again when you rest the pointer on it. It still shrinks to a thin line at the top over maximised windows.
