IBOX 2.3.0

New: IBOX account (Telegram)
- Sign in with Telegram, then manage your Nex subscription inside IBOX, with no mini app needed.
- Music player in IBOX, with background playback on Android.

New: Nex and Amnezia
- Nex servers and subscriptions carry a clear Nex badge, and the home screen shows "Nex active" while connected.
- Nex AmneziaWG keys open straight in AmneziaVPN. IBOX notices when AmneziaVPN is connected to one of our servers.

New: Automations and rules
- Opt-in automations: connection watchdog, trusted Wi-Fi, nightly subscription refresh and a quota watcher.
- Rules for individual apps on Android, and rules now work the same way on Xray servers.
- New settings pages for automations and rules on Windows, Linux and Android.

New: Privacy
- The time zone can follow your exit country.
- Fewer signs that give away a VPN, and a warning when other apps detect our servers.

New: Mini view (Windows)
- A small window with a server picker.

IBAR
- The notch can float as an island, and music comes first, with seeking.
- Clicks pass through the notch, it shrinks to a thin line over maximised windows, and it shows a focus timer and system vitals.
- Batteries of connected Bluetooth devices.
- Provider news, with "mark as read".
- Claude permission prompts show Persian text, emoji and tool input correctly, and approve/deny is more reliable.

Faster
- Windows uses about 1% CPU while minimised (it was 4–11%). Android stops its animations in the background.
- The Android power button no longer stays pink after a state change.
