    How it works

    A rule matches when any of its destination conditions (domains, IPs, country lists, rule-sets) matches and each of its other conditions (apps, ports, protocol) matches. Each rule leads to one place: through the VPN, direct, or blocked. Saving applies to the running connection at once.
